Support

Sequence, on the web, for Mac, and for iPhone

Questions, bug reports, or ideas? Email hi@sequence.photos. It helps to say whether you're using the website, the Mac app, or the iPhone app, and which version (on the Mac, in the About pane).

Where's my work saved?

On the website, in your browser's storage on this device. Use File → Export project to keep a copy, or to move it to another browser or computer. In the Mac app, in the project file you save (File → Save). On iPhone, in the app, on that phone: export your frames to keep a copy, since deleting the app deletes them.

What does it need?

The website works in current versions of Chrome, Edge, Safari, and Firefox, and keeps working offline once it's loaded. The Mac app needs macOS 13 or later, and the iPhone app iOS 17 or later.
