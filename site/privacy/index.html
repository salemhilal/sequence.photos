Privacy Policy

Sequence, on the web and for Mac · Updated October 18, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is handled in Sequence, both the website (%VITE_SITE_DOMAIN%) and the Mac app.

We collect no personal information

We don’t collect, save, or have access to any of your personal data. The photos you add and the projects you make stay on your device: in your browser’s storage on the website, and in the app and the files you save on the Mac. Everything the app does with your photos, including making previews, PDFs, images, and InDesign exports, happens on your device. Unless you post to Instagram (below), nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Your settings, like the theme and page size, are stored only on your device.

The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking. Unless you connect Instagram, it makes no requests to third parties. Like any website, the site’s host (Netlify) may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, as part of serving it.

We do not and will never sell any data.

Posting to Instagram

Posting to Instagram is optional. None of what follows happens unless you connect an Instagram account in Sequence’s settings.

Connecting. You sign in on Instagram’s own site. Sequence’s server exchanges the sign-in for an access token and passes it back to the app, along with your Instagram username and account type. The token and username are kept only on your device: in your browser’s storage on the website, and in the app on the Mac. The server doesn’t keep them, and has no accounts or database.

Posting. Instagram only accepts photos it can download from a web address, so when you post, the app uploads that post’s images to Sequence’s server (stored with Cloudflare). Instagram downloads them from there. The app sends your caption, and the request to publish, to Instagram directly, not through our server. The uploaded images are deleted as soon as the post is published, and automatically within about an hour and a half if it isn’t. They’re never used for anything else.

Server logs. To keep the service working, the server logs each request with no images, tokens, captions, or web addresses that grant access to your images. To tell requests apart, it logs a scrambled form of your Instagram account number that can’t be matched back to your account. These logs are kept for 3 days. Your IP address is used briefly to limit abuse and isn’t stored.

Usage counts. So we know how Sequence is used, the server counts sign-ins, uploads, and whether each post went through, with the same scrambled account number and nothing else about you or your images. These counts are kept for 3 months.

What you post is subject to Instagram’s privacy policy.

Disconnecting Instagram and deleting your data

To disconnect, choose Disconnect in Sequence’s settings, which removes the token from your device. To revoke Sequence’s access completely, remove it in Instagram under Settings and activity → App website permissions → Apps and websites. Since our server keeps no personal data, there’s nothing else to delete. Any images still waiting to be posted are deleted automatically within about an hour and a half. If you’d like us to confirm this, email us.

Children’s privacy

Sequence complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act: it doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
