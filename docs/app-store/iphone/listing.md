## Description (4,000 characters)

Sequence is for the part of photography that happens after the shoot: deciding what goes next to what.

Bring in your photos and they each get a frame. See the whole run at once, drag frames into a new order, and swipe through a full-size preview to feel how it plays.

LAY OUT EVERY FRAME
• Move and resize photos with your finger, snapping to the frame's edges and guides
• Keep photos on the desk until you know where they belong, then drag them onto a frame
• Pinch to zoom in for careful placement
• Set a white or black background for a frame, or for all of them
• Add a white or black border around a photo
• Undo and redo anything

CROP WITHOUT LOSING YOUR PLACE
• Crop to a common shape or freely
• Pin the crop to hold it still and move the photo inside it
• Sequence warns you when a photo is enlarged past its resolution, before it shows in your export

MADE FOR THE SIZES YOU POST
• Start from Instagram's sizes or slides, or set a custom size
• Duplicate, delete, and reorder several frames at once

SHARE IT
• Export JPEG or PNG images of all your frames, or just the ones you choose
• Save them to Photos or send them anywhere with the share sheet
• Post to Instagram as a single photo, a carousel, or stories, and see how it will look first (posting needs an Instagram Business or Creator account)

YOUR PHOTOS STAY YOURS
There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking. Your photos and sequences stay on your iPhone. If you post to Instagram, that post's images pass through Sequence's server so Instagram can fetch them, and they're deleted as soon as it's published.

Sequence is also on the web and the Mac, where you can lay out books and export PDFs and InDesign files.

## What to test (TestFlight)

Start a sequence and add a few photos. Rearrange frames in the grid, open a frame, move and resize its photos, drag one to and from the desk, crop one, and preview the sequence. Export images to Photos. If you have an Instagram Business or Creator account, try a post to a test account.
